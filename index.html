About Kaafiye Veterinary Pharmacy

Dedicated to Clinical Standards & Pastoral Prosperity

Located on Road No. 1 in Barwaaqo, Berbera, Kaafiye Veterinary Pharmacy is a trusted veterinary pharmacy and animal health center serving pastoralists, dairy farmers, and livestock exporters across the Sahil region.

We combine strict cold-chain pharmaceutical storage with practical clinical guidance—ensuring every herd receives authentic medicines, accurate diagnostics, and responsible dosage protocols that safeguard animal welfare and food safety.

Clinical & Pharmacy Services

Comprehensive Animal Health Care

Professional veterinary pharmacy dispensing, laboratory diagnostics, surgical care, and field support under one roof.

Veterinary Pharmacy & Dispensary

Genuine vaccines, antibiotics, dewormers, acaricides, and essential vitamins dispensed with clear species-specific dosage and withdrawal period guidance.

Laboratory Analysis & Diagnostics

Fecal parasitology, blood smear microscopy, milk mastitis screening, and rapid infectious disease diagnostics for accurate treatment plans.

Veterinary Surgery & Clinical Care

Minor and major surgical procedures, abscess drainage, wound suturing, hoof care, and emergency obstetric assistance for calving and kidding.

Dairy & Herd Productivity Support

Mastitis prevention protocols, mineral supplementation, fertility support, and practical dairy nutrition planning to maximize healthy milk yield.

Field Visits & Herd Vaccination

Scheduled on-site farm and pastoral herd health visits, seasonal mass vaccination campaigns, and outbreak response across Berbera.

Walk-in Consultation & Fast Delivery
